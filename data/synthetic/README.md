# Synthetic / augmented data

Anything generated or augmented lives here, never in `manual/`, so the 500-sample count stays
auditable. Files are kept on the team Drive, not in the public repo.

| File | Generated by | Seed | Method |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `contract_span_samples.csv` | `scripts/build_dataset.py` (`pvf.data.augment`) | `project.seed` = 42 | Contract-span expansion |

**Contract-span expansion.** A hand-collected contract also covers the other 1 July dates between
its start and end. Each such date becomes a sample with the same contract and its own anchor.
Dates are dropped when the player had changed club, or when the one-year target runs past the
data. A seeded sample of the rest tops the dataset up to `augment.target_total` (1,000). Each row
keeps its parent `rank`. These rows are used for training only and are never scored. Full
reasoning and the known blind spot (extensions we did not see) are in `docs/scope_change.md`.
